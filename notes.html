01 · Creative decisions

One idea, carried through every scroll.

THE HERO

Type that sits behind the subject

The hero portrait is cut out so the oversized wordmark sits behind her hair. It is a depth effect that feels cinematic and editorial, and it only works because the photography is this strong. A moving “light” brightens the letters as you move across the page, a literal version of what Joshua teaches.

THE RHYTHM

Dark hero, ivory content, dark again

Exactly the rhythm in the brand book. Obsidian frames the dramatic work; ivory makes courses, journal and learning content easy to read. Terracotta is used only for actions and small marks, never as a large block.

THE PHOTOGRAPHY

Teach through the pictures

“Four ways to shape light” turns the brand book’s art-direction categories (rim light, patterned shadows, texture and colour, gels) into an interactive lesson. Visitors learn something in the first 30 seconds, which shows the teaching style before they buy.

THE EXPERIENCE

Immersive, but never in the way

A short loader, a drifting light, scroll-filled manifesto text, a draggable course rail, a parallax member wall and a full-screen mobile menu. Everything respects prefers-reduced-motion and stays keyboard accessible.

THE BRAND

Brand book, followed

Only the five approved colours. Poppins 400/500 for body and UI. Headings use TT Interphases’ role, with Inter Tight as a stand-in until licensed files arrive. British English, “you”, action-led buttons, and no invented prices, dates or statistics.

THE ARCHITECTURE

Education brand, with an endorsement

“By Joshua Sokoya” appears as a quiet endorsement, not a second logo, and a dedicated section sends qualified visitors to joshuasokoya.com. This keeps the two brands distinct but connected.

What I moved away from

  • Template-style card grids with stock-like icon rows
  • Small hero image with text crowding the subject
  • Every section looking the same
  • Prices and dates on a design that cannot confirm them

What this concept does instead

  • One commanding image with scale, negative space and depth
  • Varied section formats: rail, schedule, wall, lesson stage
  • Photography as the identity, cropped around the eyes
  • Honest “coming soon” states that still capture leads

CURIOUS BEGINNER

PROGRESSING PORTRAIT PHOTOGRAPHER

The lighting studio, featured course and workshop schedule speak directly to people who already shoot and want control of light.

04 · Building it in WordPress

Fast, editable, and easy to grow.

FOUNDATION

Custom block theme

  • Lightweight block (full-site-editing) theme. Brand tokens (colours, type scale, spacing) live in theme.json, so styles stay consistent.
  • Reusable patterns for hero, course rail, schedule, wall, CTA bands; the team edits content without touching code.
  • ACF Pro (or native block.json) for custom fields on courses and workshops.
  • Fonts self-hosted (Poppins, licensed TT Interphases); no render-blocking third-party requests.
CONTENT MODEL

Structured, filterable content

  • Custom post types: Course, Workshop, Resource; Journal uses standard posts.
  • Taxonomies: category, level, format, so the catalogue filters instantly.
  • Level, duration and price fields show only when filled, matching the brand rule “only when confirmed”.
  • Rank Math with Course, Event, Product and Organisation schema for rich results.
COMMERCE & LEARNING

Right tool per job

  • Courses: LearnDash or Tutor LMS (to be chosen with Joshua based on drip, certificates and budget).
  • Digital products, presets, merch: WooCommerce with Stripe/PayPal; digital downloads and later print-on-demand.
  • Workshops: Events Calendar + Event Tickets, or WooCommerce Bookings, with waitlist, deposits and capacity limits.
  • Community stays on Skool, linked and tracked.
PERFORMANCE

Cinematic, still fast

  • Responsive WebP/AVIF with srcset; hero preloaded; below-fold lazy-loaded.
  • Motion in a small vanilla-JS module loaded on the homepage only. No heavy page-builder bundle.
  • Full-page caching and CDN (WP Rocket or LiteSpeed plus Cloudflare). Targets: green Core Web Vitals on mobile.
MARKETING STACK

Measure everything

  • GA4 + GTM with events for course views, add-to-cart, waitlist, Skool clicks and portfolio clicks.
  • Email via Kit/ConvertKit, MailerLite or FluentCRM with tags per interest (courses, workshops, community).
  • Meta pixel and conversions API for retargeting, behind consent.
PROCESS

Safe to ship, safe to change

  • Staging environment; Git-based deploys; test checkout and forms before launch.
  • Accessibility pass (contrast, focus states, alt text, reduced motion).
  • Security and backups: Cloudflare, Wordfence or similar, daily offsite backups.
  • Handover docs and short video walkthroughs for the team.

05 · Helping the business grow

Every section has a job.

COURSE SALES

Show the teaching, then sell it

  • A featured course with a clear outcome in the first scroll.
  • Free preview lessons and journal tutorials feed an email list that is nurtured towards the course.
  • Student work and verified testimonials added as soon as they exist, never invented.
  • Bundles, early-bird launches and retargeting for course-page visitors.
WORKSHOP BOOKINGS

Capture demand before dates exist

  • The waitlist works now and builds a ready audience for each release.
  • Clear location, inclusions and accessibility information remove booking friction.
  • Honest scarcity (capacity from the event system), deposit option and an email sequence leading up to release.
  • Past-workshop galleries become social proof.
COMMUNITY

Make joining feel like belonging

  • Member work wall and monthly challenges show life inside, before the click.
  • A tracked link into Skool, with a free tier or challenge as a low-commitment doorway if Joshua wants one.
  • Members become the pipeline for courses and workshops, and the source of content.
TWO BRANDS, ONE FLYWHEEL

Education feeds commercial, commercial feeds education

Behind-the-scenes from commercial shoots becomes journal content and course case studies. The “working photographer” credibility sends students to Photography Here, while the About section sends serious prospects to joshuasokoya.com. Shared analytics tracks both directions.

WHAT I WOULD MEASURE

Simple KPIs, reviewed monthly

Email sign-ups per 100 visitors, course page → checkout rate, workshop waitlist size and conversion to booking, Skool joins from the site, returning visitors, Core Web Vitals. Targets are set after a baseline month, not guessed.
